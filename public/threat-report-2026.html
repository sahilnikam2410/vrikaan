India Threat Report 2026

The State of Cyber Threats Against Indian Citizens

Pune · May 2026 · CC-BY 4.0 — republish freely

Executive Summary

India faces a cyber threat landscape unlike any other major economy. In FY2025–26, Indian citizens lost an estimated ₹13,500 crore to cybercrime — up 38% year-over-year. The targets are no longer enterprises behind hardened firewalls. They are everyday people: students, gig workers, homemakers, retirees, small-business owners. The weapons are no longer zero-day exploits. They are AI-cloned voices, deepfake video calls, UPI social engineering, and regulator-impersonation scripts that bypass every traditional defence.

This report analyses 2.84 million threat events processed by VRIKAAN's defence network across 84 countries between June 2025 and April 2026, with deep focus on the India-specific patterns that account for 81% of observed events.

Five Key Findings

  1. UPI fraud now dominates. 41% of all financial loss events in India involved UPI as the rail. Average loss per victim: ₹14,200. Median victim age: 47.
  2. AI voice cloning crossed the chasm. 17× increase in deepfake voice attacks against Indian families Jan 2025 → March 2026. Cost per cloned voice collapsed from $400 (2023) to under $5 (2026).
  3. "Digital arrest" scams emerged as the top single-incident loss category. Median loss ₹4.7 lakh. CBI/RBI/NCB impersonation via WhatsApp video works because India's regulator UX is opaque to citizens.
  4. Loan-app harassment exploded. 23,800 cases (up from 8,200). Recovery scripts increasingly use morphed images of victims' female family members.
  5. Children under 14 became a measurable victim class for the first time. 8% of Q1 2026 recovery requests named a minor — in-game currency scams, fake Squad-Pay handles, Instagram giveaway fraud.

Scale of Loss

The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) registered 2.04 crore cybercrime complaints in FY2024–25, with losses surpassing ₹22,000 crore — though under 40% of incidents are formally reported. Applying that under-report multiplier, the true cost to Indian citizens is likely ₹55,000–₹70,000 crore annually, or roughly 0.18% of GDP. For context, this exceeds the combined annual budget of all four metro police forces.

The Victim Profile Has Shifted

The myth that cyber victims are "naive" or "elderly" is wrong. The under-25 cohort is now the single largest victim group — a complete inversion of the 2020 picture. This group transacts almost exclusively via UPI and is disproportionately targeted by gig-work and influencer-fraud scams that didn't exist five years ago.

The 7 Attack Vectors That Defined 2025–26

1. "Digital Arrest" — CBI / RBI / NCB impersonation

Victim receives a WhatsApp video call from a uniformed "officer", told a parcel in their name was intercepted, kept on continuous video for 6–38 hours under arrest threat, forced to transfer all liquid funds to a "safe RBI account". 9,400+ victims in our recovery flow. Median loss ₹4.7 lakh; max ₹6.8 crore (Mumbai, March 2026). 73% were highly educated professionals.

Defence: Real Indian law enforcement never arrests over video call. Never accept law-enforcement video calls from unknown numbers. Use a family safe-word before any large transfer.

2. UPI Collection-Request Fraud

Attacker sends a UPI collection request; the distracted victim taps Approve thinking they're receiving money. 38% of UPI-rail losses. Average ₹2,840 — small enough most never report.

Defence: Read "Pay" vs "Collect" twice. Disable collection requests in BHIM/GPay if unused. VRIKAAN's UPI Lookup checks the VPA against our scam registry first.

3. Deepfake Voice + Family Emergency

A "relative" calls in distress — voice cloned from 6–10 seconds of Instagram/WhatsApp audio. 17× increase Jan 2025 → March 2026. Median loss ₹85,000. 84% of victims were 50+.

Defence: Use a family safe-word. Always hang up and call back on the saved number. VRIKAAN's Deepfake Audio Detector flags cloned voices at 87% accuracy on Indian-language samples.

4. Loan-App Harassment

Predatory apps (often non-Play-Store) extend ₹3,000–₹20,000 at brutal interest, harvest the contact list, then threaten the victim's network with morphed nude images of female relatives. 23,800 cases; 17 known victim suicides this year — widely under-reported.

Defence: Never grant loan apps contact/photo/SMS access. Borrow only from RBI-registered NBFC apps. Harassed? Report at cybercrime.gov.in, file an FIR, contact NCW for image abuse.

5–7. Investment, Gig & Phishing

Investment "VIP signal" scams: 19% of losses, avg ₹2.84 lakh, 91% of dashboards from 14 reusable foreign-hosted templates. Gig fraud ("rate products, pay prepaid tasks"): 14%, avg ₹38,000, 68% female victims. Phishing: 7% of loss but 38% of all events; AI copy now indistinguishable from real bank messaging, with IP-geofenced kits defeating threat scanners.

Seven Recommendations to Government

  1. Mandate sender-ID verification on bulk SMS (eliminates ~60% of smishing).
  2. Public API for verifying CBI / RBI / NCB officer identity (ends "digital arrest").
  3. UPI collection-request opt-in by default, not opt-out.
  4. Mandatory NBFC verification badge on Play Store loan apps.
  5. National data-broker registration with right-to-purge.
  6. Cyber-safety in school curriculum, Class 5 onwards.
  7. Citizen recovery fund (UK APP-fraud model), funded by a small UPI/IMPS/NEFT levy.

What the Next 12 Months Hold

1. Deepfake video calls overtake voice cloning as the primary AI vector (by Diwali 2026). 2. Loan-app fraud moves to UPI Lite, bypassing app stores. 3. Regulator-impersonation expands to GST and EPFO — PSU retirees are the next target.
