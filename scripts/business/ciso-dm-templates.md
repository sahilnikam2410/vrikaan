# VRIKAAN — CISO Outreach DM Templates

Cold LinkedIn DMs to CISOs / CTOs / VP Eng at mid-market Indian companies.

---

## RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

1. **Personalize the first line.** Reference a recent funding round, hire, talk, blog post — anything that proves you read their profile.
2. **No pitch deck on first message.** Goal = 20-min conversation, not contract.
3. **One ask per message.** Either "open to a 20-min chat?" or "thoughts on X?". Never both.
4. **Don't follow up more than once.** If they ignore twice, move on. Re-add to list in 6 months.
5. **Send Tue-Thu, 9-11 AM IST OR 6-8 PM IST.** Avoids Monday inbox storm + Friday-evening dead zone.
6. **Use your personal LinkedIn (Sahil), not brand account.** Founder DMs convert 5-10× higher than brand DMs.
7. **Track every send in a spreadsheet:** name · company · industry · date sent · reply (Y/N) · meeting booked (Y/N).

## RESEARCH CHECKLIST — Before sending any DM

| Spend 5 min checking these on the prospect's LinkedIn + company site | Why |
|---|---|
| Their last 3 LinkedIn posts | Use for personalized opener |
| Their company's About / press section | Find recent funding / launch / partnership |
| Their company's tech stack (built with / StackShare) | Tailor SOC-Pro vs SOC-Enterprise pitch |
| Their compliance certifications (ISO, SOC 2, PCI) on their site footer | Cite during conversation |
| Their CISO predecessor (if recently hired) | Reference: "you joined N months ago, congrats" |
| Their open security job postings | "Saw you're hiring SOC analysts — happy to fill the gap with managed service" |
| Their press around any breach / fine / inspection finding | Mention CAREFULLY — only if public |

Do NOT mention competitor names, internal employees, or anything not publicly available.

## ANTI-PATTERNS — what kills replies

- ❌ "Hope this finds you well" (instant delete)
- ❌ Mass-cold-emailed via tool (Outreach, Apollo) — looks low-effort
- ❌ Long messages > 150 words
- ❌ Pitching specific pricing in DM #1 (saves for call)
- ❌ Pitching multiple tiers in one DM (confuses prospect)
- ❌ Name-dropping customers under NDA
- ❌ Using "synergy", "leverage", "best-in-class", "next-gen" — corporate buzzword bingo
- ❌ Asking for "30 minutes to learn about your business" (they're busy, you need to bring value first)
- ❌ Sending Friday evening / weekend / Indian public holiday
- ❌ Following up more than ONCE
