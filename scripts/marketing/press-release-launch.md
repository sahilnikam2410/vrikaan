### Nashik-based startup tackles India's ₹11,000 Cr+ cyber-scam epidemic with a free, India-first alternative to Norton and McAfee

**NASHIK, India — [Insert date], 2026 —** Indian cybersecurity startup **VRIKAAN** today announced the public launch of its AI-powered cyber defense platform at [vrikaan.com](https://vrikaan.com), bringing together 50+ security tools — including scam-SMS detection, deepfake audio analysis, MITRE ATT&CK-mapped SOC dashboard, vulnerability scanning, and dark-web monitoring — under one free login. India lost an estimated **₹11,000 crore to cyber scams in 2024**, with rural and tier-2 users disproportionately affected by UPI fraud, fake KYC, vishing calls, and AI-driven voice-clone scams. Existing solutions — Norton, McAfee, Kaspersky — are English-only, cost ₹2,000+ per year, and ship as antivirus-first products that don't address India's actual threat surface.

VRIKAAN's free tier covers all 50+ tools, including a SOC dashboard that mirrors enterprise tools like Wazuh with real-time MITRE ATT&CK tactic mapping and compliance posture tracking across PCI DSS, GDPR, DPDP, SOC 2, and ISO 27001. A ₹990-per-year Pro plan unlocks unlimited AI scans, priority dark-web monitoring, and PDF exports. Payments are processed natively in INR via Cashfree (UPI, cards, netbanking).

### Built by SOC analysts, not just engineers

VRIKAAN was co-founded in 2024 by **Sahil Anil Nikam** (Founder & CEO — SOC Analyst & Cybersecurity Researcher) and **Khushi Ishwar Raigade** (Co-Founder — SOC Analyst & Cybersecurity Researcher). Both work daily inside live security operations centers, mapping threats to the MITRE ATT&CK framework and writing the detection rules that power the platform.

*"Cybersecurity shouldn't be a luxury — it should be a right available to every person on Earth,"* said **Sahil Anil Nikam**, Founder & CEO. *"My grandmother shouldn't need a ₹2,000 subscription and English fluency to know if her UPI payment is a scam. We built VRIKAAN so that defense scales like the offense already does."*

*"Detection beats reaction. A defender's job is to find the attacker before they find the data,"* added **Khushi Ishwar Raigade**, Co-Founder. *"That's why we built a real MITRE ATT&CK-mapped SOC into the free tier — not just a slogan, but an actual heatmap, tactic by tactic, technique by technique."*

### Technology that runs on ₹0 infrastructure

VRIKAAN runs entirely on free-tier serverless infrastructure (12 of 12 Vercel hobby-tier functions used), shipping 109 automated tests, and is powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash for AI-driven scam classification, deepfake-audio detection, and vulnerability synthesis. The platform also ships a browser extension (Chrome/Edge, Manifest V3), a Wazuh-style SOC dashboard, custom RFC 6238 TOTP two-factor authentication (NIST Authenticator Assurance Level 2), and HMAC-SHA256-signed outbound webhooks for enterprise integrations.

5. Tag the exclusive outlet from your X / LinkedIn launch posts

Exclusives 2-3x your first-day reach vs simultaneous mass-pitch. Worth it if you're under 1k followers.

## ❌ WHAT NOT TO DO

- ❌ Don't claim "first ever" or "biggest" unless verifiable — journalists fact-check, you'll get burned
- ❌ Don't send the same email to multiple journalists at the same outlet — looks lazy
- ❌ Don't follow up more than once. Silence = no.
- ❌ Don't attach PDFs to first email — links to vrikaan.com/press only
- ❌ Don't pitch on Mondays (inbox flood) or Fridays (no one's reading). Tue/Wed/Thu best
- ❌ Don't pitch in December or during major Indian festivals (Diwali week, Holi week)
