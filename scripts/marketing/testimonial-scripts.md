# VRIKAAN — Testimonial Video Script (Remote, Home Shoots)

**Featuring:** Sahil Anil Nikam (PRN 220105131264) & Ankalesh Dipak Somvanshi (PRN 220105131273)
**College:** Sandip University, Nashik · Department of Computer Science and Engineering
**Project:** VRIKAAN — AI-Powered Cybersecurity Assistance and Monitoring Platform
**Live at:** vrikaan.com

**Format:** ONE final video. Both speakers record at their own homes, separately. No on-camera meeting. Edit cuts between them. Sahil's half explains the project's *what & why*. Ankalesh's half explains the *engineering & impact*.

**Final length:** ~60–65 seconds. Each person speaks ~30 seconds + a 5s shared end card (recorded separately, voice-over).

---

## SHOOT INDEPENDENTLY · EDIT TOGETHER

Each person shoots their own portion at home:
- Phone or laptop webcam (1080p minimum, 4K preferred)
- Quiet room, plain wall behind, soft natural light from a window in front
- Lavalier mic OR clean phone audio with no echo
- Look just left/right of the lens (not directly into it) — feels like an interview, not a piece-to-camera

Then editor stitches both takes together with B-roll between.

## DIRECTOR'S NOTES

1. **Match the look.** Both shoots must use the same aspect ratio, similar lighting direction, and similar framing. If Sahil shoots in portrait 9:16 close-up, Ankalesh must do the same. Any mismatch will make the edit feel cheap.
2. **Sahil's strongest line** is "Hindi or English. The same product." Read it slowly.
3. **Ankalesh's strongest line** is "Twelve out of twelve. Zero rupees of infrastructure cost." Land it.
4. The closing trio — *"Two students. One free tier. One real product."* — is best delivered by **Ankalesh alone** in the current draft, since you're not together. Read it medium-pace, with a half-beat of silence after each line.
