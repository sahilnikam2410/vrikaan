## The one-line pitch
**VRIKAAN Scam DNA — India's first live, crowdsourced scam-immune system.** Paste any suspicious message and instantly see if others are getting the same scam. An AI scambaiter wastes scammers' time and harvests their playbook to protect everyone.

## Why it's novel (the wedge)
- Antiviruses scan files. We built a **living network of scam fingerprints** — every check makes it smarter (network effect = the moat).
- An **AI honeypot** that talks back to scammers in Hinglish, stalls them, and extracts their UPI IDs + scripts → auto-feeds the network.
- **Family mesh:** one member hits a scam → the whole family gets a heads-up.
- 100% India-first: UPI fraud, digital arrest, KYC, fake courier, TRAI SIM scams.
