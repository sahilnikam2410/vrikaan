## 1. Co-Founder Agreement (term sheet → full agreement)

Sign this **before** incorporation (or immediately after) so equity, roles, and
IP are unambiguous. A CS can convert this into a formal Founders' Agreement +
Shareholders' Agreement (SHA).

### 1.1 Parties & roles
| Founder | Role / Title | Primary ownership |
|---|---|---|
| Sahil Anil Nikam | Founder & CEO | Product, engineering, security research, fundraising |
| Khushi Ishwar Raigade | Co-Founder & COO/CISO | Operations, SOC/compliance, partnerships |

> Decide titles deliberately — they signal responsibility to customers/investors.

### 1.2 Equity split
- Proposed: **__ % / __ %** (e.g. 60/40 or 50/50). Write the actual number here.
- **Why not auto-50/50:** split by contribution (idea, time, capital, risk,
  domain). Unequal but fair beats equal-but-resentful.
- Record share count, not just %, after incorporation (e.g. 6,000 / 4,000 of
  10,000 shares at ₹10 face value).

### 1.3 Vesting (critical — protects both of you)
- **4-year vesting, 1-year cliff.** Nothing vests before 12 months; then monthly
  or quarterly over the remaining 3 years.
- Applies to **both** founders, including shares already "owned" — implemented as
  the company's right to **buy back unvested shares at face value** if a founder
  leaves early (a "reverse vesting" / restricted-share clause in the SHA).
- **Accelerated vesting** on acquisition (single or double-trigger) — define it.

### 1.4 Leaver clauses
- **Good leaver** (death, disability, mutual exit): keeps vested shares.
- **Bad leaver** (resignation before cliff, termination for cause, competing):
  company/other founder can buy back at face value or fair value — define.
- **Drag-along / tag-along** rights for future investor exits.

### 1.5 IP assignment (do NOT skip)
- All IP created by either founder for VRIKAAN (code, brand, designs, the
  Vrikaan fonts/marks, tools, content) is **assigned to the company**.
- Any pre-existing personal IP used must be licensed/assigned in writing.

### 1.6 Roles, time commitment, decisions
- Full-time vs part-time per founder; minimum hours/week.
- **Reserved matters** needing both founders' consent: raising money, issuing
  shares, taking debt > ₹__, hiring C-level, selling the company, changing
  business, spending > ₹__ single item.
- Day-to-day: each founder decides within their domain.
- **Deadlock resolution:** mediation → defined tie-breaker (e.g. CEO casts
  deciding vote on ops; board on strategic).

### 1.7 Compensation & expenses
- Founder salaries (when affordable), expense policy, no surprise withdrawals.

### 1.8 Confidentiality & non-compete
- Mutual NDA; non-solicit of staff/customers for __ months post-exit;
  reasonable non-compete (keep enforceable — courts in India dislike broad ones).

## 2. ESOP Pool (Employee Stock Option Plan)

For hiring talent without cash. Set up at or soon after incorporation.

- **Pool size:** carve **10–15%** of fully-diluted equity into an ESOP pool.
- **Dilution:** comes from *both* founders pro-rata (everyone shares the cost).
- **Per-hire vesting:** 4-year, 1-year cliff (mirror founder vesting).
- **Exercise price:** at/near fair market value (FMV) at grant; get a valuation.
- **Pool mechanics:** create an "ESOP trust" or direct grants; CS sets up the
  scheme + board/shareholder approval (Companies Act s.62 + SEBI/Rule 12 for
  unlisted private cos).
- **Grant ranges (typical, fully-diluted):**
  | Hire | Range |
  |---|---|
  | First 5 employees | 0.5% – 2% each |
  | Senior eng / lead | 0.25% – 1% |
  | Early non-exec | 0.05% – 0.25% |
- Keep a **cap table** spreadsheet from day one (founders + pool + future
  investors). Update on every grant/raise.

## 3. Private Limited Incorporation Checklist (MCA, India)

A Pvt Ltd is the right vehicle for raising money + ESOPs + credibility.

### 3.1 Pre-requisites (both founders/directors)
- [ ] **DSC** (Digital Signature Certificate) — Class 3, for each director (~₹1–2k each).
- [ ] **DIN** (Director Identification Number) — auto-allotted via SPICe+ for first directors.
- [ ] PAN + Aadhaar (linked), passport-size photo, email, mobile.
- [ ] Address proof: bank statement / utility bill (≤2 months old).
- [ ] Registered office proof: rent agreement + NOC from owner, or owned-property docs + latest utility bill.

### 3.2 Name (decide before filing)
- [ ] Reserve via **SPICe+ Part A** (or RUN). Proposed: **VRIKAAN TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED** (have 1–2 backups).
- [ ] Check name availability on MCA + trademark (IP India) so brand ≠ taken.
- [ ] Objects must cover: cybersecurity software, SaaS, IT services.

### 3.3 Filing (single integrated form: **SPICe+**)
- [ ] **SPICe+ Part B** — incorporation.
- [ ] **e-MOA (INC-33)** + **e-AOA (INC-34)** — see §4.
- [ ] **AGILE-PRO-S** — GSTIN (optional now), EPFO, ESIC, profession tax (state), bank account.
- [ ] Authorised + paid-up capital: e.g. authorised ₹1,00,000 (10,000 shares × ₹10), paid-up as you fund. Min paid-up no longer mandated.
- [ ] Declaration **INC-9** (auto), director consent **DIR-2**.

### 3.4 Post-incorporation (within ~30–180 days)
- [ ] **Certificate of Incorporation** + **CIN** issued.
- [ ] Company **PAN + TAN** (auto with SPICe+).
- [ ] Open **current bank account** (use AGILE-PRO-S or do separately).
- [ ] **Commencement of Business — INC-20A** within 180 days (after paying subscription money into the bank). Mandatory before borrowing.
- [ ] First **board meeting** within 30 days; appoint auditor (**ADT-1**) within 30 days.
- [ ] Issue **share certificates** within 60 days; pay stamp duty on them.
- [ ] Statutory registers + minute book.
- [ ] **Startup India / DPIIT** recognition (free) → tax + compliance benefits, easier ESOP/angel-tax exemption.

### 3.5 Indicative cost & time
- **Govt + professional fees:** ~₹7,000 – ₹15,000 (varies by state stamp duty + CA/CS fee). Maharashtra stamp duty differs from other states.
- **Time:** ~7–15 working days once documents are ready.
- **Ongoing:** annual ROC filings (AOC-4, MGT-7), ITR, audit, ~₹15–30k/yr via a CA.

## 4. MOA / AOA Notes

- **MOA (Memorandum of Association)** — the company's charter:
  - *Name, Registered office (state), Objects, Liability (limited), Capital, Subscriber* clauses.
  - **Objects** must explicitly include: developing & operating cybersecurity / fraud-detection software, SaaS, AI tools, IT-enabled services, and selling subscriptions in India and abroad.
- **AOA (Articles of Association)** — internal rulebook:
  - Adopt **Table F** as base, then customise: share transfer restrictions
    (pre-emption / right of first refusal), founder vesting & buy-back, ESOP
    authorisation, reserved matters, board composition, drag/tag-along.
  - Align AOA with the **Shareholders' Agreement** so they don't conflict.
- Get the CS to fold the co-founder agreement's vesting/leaver/reserved-matter
  terms into the **AOA + SHA** so they're legally enforceable, not just a side note.
